About

We play covers, but you wouldn't know it.

New Orleans R&B, soul, blues, and some country. The duo is Neil McCann on guitar and vocals, and Julian Smith on double bass.

The rhythm is what the guitar and the double bass do between them — one holding it down, the other pushing at it.

It works in the rooms where a full band is too much: front bars, restaurants, wineries, house parties, and the part of an evening before anyone starts dancing. Loud enough to lift the room, quiet enough that people can still hear each other order a drink.
