About

We play covers, but not the usual repertoire.

We play our favourite music our way. Soul, groove and feel with undercurrents of funk, R&B, blues and jazz, all while tapping into the roots.

Two players fill a room without crowding it: front bars, restaurants, wineries, house parties, and the part of an evening before anyone starts dancing. Enough to lift the room, and still room for people to hear each other order a drink.

Requirements

For the duo. Trio and full band on request.

Line-upTwo players — guitar and vocals, double bass
SpaceA corner will do — about 2m × 2m for the duo. We will fill a bigger stage if you have one.
PA / soundWe carry our own PA, or can use the house system. We require access to mains power.
EnergyBackground groove through to a full dance floor, set to the room
SetsThree 45-minute sets, or two 60-minute sets. Other timings by arrangement.
Set-up & pack-down30 minutes either side
TravelBased in the Northern Rivers, NSW. Can travel; costs quoted per event.
SuitsPubs, bars and restaurants · birthdays, parties and private functions · weddings · wineries · markets and local events
