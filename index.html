About

We play covers, but you wouldn't know it.

New Orleans R&B, soul, blues, and some country. The duo is Neil McCann on guitar and vocals, and Julian Smith on double bass.

These are our favourite songs, played our own way. The records are where we start, not where we finish. What carries them is the groove and the feel — soul and R&B with the roots still showing.

It works in the rooms where a full band is too much: front bars, restaurants, wineries, house parties, and the part of an evening before anyone starts dancing. Enough to lift the room, and still room for people to hear each other order a drink.
